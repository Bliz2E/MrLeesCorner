Model

  • θ is the angle between the coil plane and the field. θ = 0° means the plane is parallel to B (flux is zero, moment is greatest); θ = 90° means the plane is perpendicular to B (the commutator switches the current).
  • Coil: N = 20 turns, 10 cm along the axis, 6 cm wide, so A = 0.006 m². Speed is imposed by you; the animation plays in slow motion (set by the playback slider) so the rotation can be followed.
  • Back-emf ε = N B A ω |cos θ|, current I = (E − ε) / R, force on each side F = N B I L, moment M = N B A I |cos θ|.
  • If the back-emf exceeds the battery emf, I goes negative: the coil acts as a generator and the force opposes the rotation.
  • The commutator gaps are wider than the brushes. Within about ±11° of θ = 90° and 270° each brush sits entirely over a gap, the circuit is open and I, F and M drop to zero (shaded red in the graphs).
  • Reversing the battery or swapping the magnet poles flips the force and current directions and the rotation sense. Reversing both restores the original behaviour. The magnitudes in the graphs do not change.
