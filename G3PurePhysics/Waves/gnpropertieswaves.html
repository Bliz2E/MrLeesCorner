Formulae and facts
v = fλf = 1 / TT = 1 / fspeed = distance / time

Frequency depends on the source and does not change when a wave enters a new medium. In water, a wave slows down and its wavelength shortens when it moves into shallower water. Audible sound lies between 20 Hz and 20 kHz.
